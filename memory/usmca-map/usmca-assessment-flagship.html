Political Picture

Sheinbaum administration · Trump team · Cabinet dynamics

President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo has positioned Economy Secretary Marcelo Ebrard as the lead USMCA negotiator. Ebrard's portfolio includes his successful track record from the 2018-2020 USMCA revision, making him the natural interlocutor. The administration's strategy appears to be: offer concessions on energy market access and migration enforcement in exchange for tariff relief.

On the U.S. side, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and USTR-designate Jamieson Greer lead the review. Trump's transactional approach frames the review as validation of his trade legacy while extracting operational concessions.
