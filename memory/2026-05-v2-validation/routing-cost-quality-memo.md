### Calibration Discipline

**V4 Pro:** Uses Kent bands correctly: *Highly Likely (80-92%)*, *Chances About Even (40-62%)*, *Likely (63-79%)*. Each watch item has a band. The bands are well-anchored to evidence described in the analysis. Admiralty ratings are present (B2, C3, B3) with specific source notes. **Good.**

**Opus 4.7:** Uses Kent bands more concisely: *highly_likely (80-92%)*, *likely (63-79%)*, *chances_about_even (40-62%)*. No Admiralty ratings — the Opus brief omitted them entirely. The bands are present but less explicitly justified in the surrounding text. **Weaker — missing Admiralty ratings is a real deficit.**

**Edge: V4 Pro.** Both calibrate. V4 Pro applies Admiralty ratings per watch item. Opus omits them.

---

### Specificity of Watch Items

**V4 Pro:** Three watch items — arcabouço fiscal credibility, BCB independence stress test, external vulnerability / capital flows. Each has concrete triggers: "break above 6.0/USD", "CDS spreads above 250bps", "Treasury resorts to pedaladas fiscais" (named the specific maneuver). **Specific, actionable triggers.**

**Opus 4.7:** Three watch items — fiscal framework dilution, BCB rate path / independence vs fiscal cost, tax reform implementation. Has concrete triggers: "PLP amendment", "dividend extraction from Petrobras", "Selic between 13.50-14.25%". More emphasis on the mechanism *path* than the hard trigger levels. **Also specific, slightly different emphasis — more on political economy, less on hard thresholds.**

**Edge: V4 Pro (by a hair).** The concrete trigger levels (6.0/USD, 250bps CDS) are more directly investable.

---

### Recommendations — Actionability

**V4 Pro:** "Establish USDBRL downside puts" — generic. "Maintain dry powder for entry" — too vague for an IC. The recommendations are the weakest section: they advise caution but lack sizing, instrument selection, or exit criteria. **Generic.**

**Opus 4.7:** Three specific, sized recommendations: (1) belly-of-curve NTN-Fs with explicit 3-5% allocation target and CDS stop-loss, (2) underweight Ibovespa with three specific stock selection criteria, (3) EWZ call spreads + sovereign CDS with 80-120bps carry cost, quarterly review cadence. Each has position sizing, instruments, and an exit signal. **Actually investable.**

**Edge: Opus 4.7 (decisively).** The V4 Pro recommendations read like analyst commentary. The Opus recommendations read like an IC memo.

### Gap Acknowledgment

**V4 Pro:** "This is not a baseline crisis call" — explicitly frames what it ISN'T saying. Doesn't flag specific unknowns. **Minimal.**

**Opus 4.7:** "The arcabouço fiscal framework is being observed in real time — no historical precedent for its erosion pattern" — implicit gap. Doesn't explicitly say "we don't know how Congress will react." **Similar to V4 Pro, neither excels.**

**Edge: Neither.** Both could be stronger on gap acknowledgment.

### Source Provenance

**V4 Pro:** Three Admiralty ratings with source notes: B2 (Finance Ministry), C3 (BCB watchers), B3 (sell-side EM strategists). Each notes reliability limitations. **Consistent and meaningful.**

**Opus 4.7:** Zero Admiralty ratings. Sources referenced in text (BCB, IMF, Fazenda) but not formally rated. **Missing entirely.**

**Edge: V4 Pro.**

### Length Efficiency

**V4 Pro:** 1,555 words. BLUF is 3 paragraphs. Each watch item is ~200 words + band + rating. Recommendations are ~100 words each. **Slightly padded — recommendations could be tighter.**

**Opus 4.7:** 1,347 words. BLUF is 2 tighter paragraphs. Each watch item is ~180 words + band. Recommendations are the longest section and carry the most value. **Denser — more signal per word, but under-delivered on the 1,800-word target by 450 words.**

**Edge: Opus.** The density is higher, and the extra words went to the most valuable part (recommendations).

### Overall Verdict

| Criterion | Winner |
|---|---|
| Calibration | **V4 Pro** (Admiralty ratings, just) |
| Specificity | **V4 Pro** (harder trigger levels) |
| Recommendations | **Opus 4.7** (decisively) |
| Gap acknowledgment | Tie |
| Source provenance | **V4 Pro** (Admiralty present) |
| Length efficiency | **Opus 4.7** (denser) |

**Is Opus worth 14x the cost? On recommendations alone, yes — the V4 Pro recs were not actionable for an IC, the Opus recs were. On calibration and sourcing, V4 Pro was actually better.** The marginal dollar goes entirely into recommendation quality, which is the part of a brief a family office actually pays for.

**Recommendation:** Keep the `audience_family_office` escalation trigger. For briefs where recommendations are the primary deliverable, Opus justifies the cost. For scanning / monitoring products where calibration and sourcing matter more than trade ideas, V4 Pro is the right tier. The gate should differentiate by *deliverable type*, not just audience label.
