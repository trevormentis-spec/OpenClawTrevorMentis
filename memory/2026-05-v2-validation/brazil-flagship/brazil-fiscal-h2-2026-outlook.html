Scenario Framework: Four Paths

Structure Around Two Critical Uncertainties

  • BASE (45% prob): Fiscal framework holds with automatic triggers, Lula or opposition win. Selic 11.25% end-2027, debt 99% GDP, growth 1.7-2.0%.
  • UPSIDE (15% prob): Reform breakthrough — post-election coalition strengthens fiscal consolidation. Selic below 10%, debt stabilizes ~95%, growth 2.5-3%.
  • DOWNSIDE (30% prob): Fiscal drift — framework weakened, primary deficit persists at 0.5%. Selic above 12.5%, debt 100%+, growth below 1.5%.
  • TAIL (10% prob): Fiscal crisis — contested election + external shock. Selic near 15%, debt 105%+, GDP contraction, multi-notch downgrade, IMF intervention.
  • Key discriminator: The primary balance + post-election Congressional composition + 6-month inflation breakevens
