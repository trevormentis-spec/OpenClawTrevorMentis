## PART D — Anti-Recommendations

**DO NOT SUBSCRIBE:**

1. **Stratfor** — Expensive ($299/yr), analysis quality has declined significantly since founding team left. Free alternatives (Cipher Brief, GZERO) are equal or better.
2. **The Economist Intelligence Unit** — Massive overlap with existing sources. Low signal-to-noise ratio for the price point.
3. **DefenseOne Daily** — You already have Breaking Defense Daily + Weekly. DefenseOne offers marginal differentiation.
4. **Council on Foreign Relations Daily Roundup** — Too general/academic. Not operational enough for brief construction.
5. **MIT Technology Review** — Consumer-oriented, lacks defense/security lens on technology.
6. **Axios Newsletters** — Surface-level analysis optimized for brevity over insight. Won't produce anything you can't get faster from Reuters/Bloomberg.
7. **The Intercept** — Strong investigative journalism but partisan framing requires too much contextual filtering for brief use.
