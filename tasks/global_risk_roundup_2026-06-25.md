**Status:** Fragile. Agreed 19 June via US-Qatar-Iran mediation. Renewed after 24 hours of intense violence threatened to collapse the Iran deal framework.

**Key developments:**
- **19 June** — Israel and Hezbollah agreed to renew ceasefire after deadly flareup that posed early challenge to US-Iran agreement. (Guardian)
- **20 June** — Iran threatened to close Strait of Hormuz over Israeli strikes in Lebanon, then pulled back. (Guardian)
- **Status unclear:** Hezbollah stated it would abide by a ceasefire if Israel does, but stopped short of confirming one is in place. (Guardian, 20 June)
- **Peace talks ongoing:** 4th round of Israel-Lebanon peace talks held in Washington DC (2-3 June), covering IDF withdrawal, LAF takeover of Southern Lebanon, Hezbollah disarmament, enforcement mechanisms. (Wikipedia)

**⚠️ Assessment:** Ceasefire is brittle. Hezbollah's ambiguity is deliberate — reserving the right to respond to Israeli operations. The Lebanon track is now the most likely flashpoint that could unravel the broader US-Iran architecture.

**Significance:**

- Demonstrates JNIM capability to project power into "secure" urban zones
- JNIM has claimed ~1,000 killed across the Sahel since April, primarily in Burkina Faso
- Follows ~280 attacks in Burkina Faso in H1 2025 (double 2024 rate)

**⚠️ Assessment:** This is a regional inflection point. JNIM is executing deliberate urban escalation. Expect increased attacks on government/security infrastructure in Niamey and Bamako. The Sahel is moving from rural insurgency to hybrid urban-rural campaign.
