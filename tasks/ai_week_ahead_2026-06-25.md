## 1. 🚨 LEAD STORY: Anthropic Export Control Crisis — Fable 5 & Mythos 5

### What Happened

- **9 June** — Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5, representing a significant capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8.
- **12 June, 5:21pm ET** — US Commerce Department issued an urgent export-control directive citing national security concerns, barring foreign national access.
- Anthropic disabled both models *globally* — the only way to comply in real time.
- **19 June** — Trump told Axios he no longer views Anthropic as a national security threat, saying the company "behaved very responsibly."
- **Status: Fable 5 still suspended** as of 25 June. Anthropic says restoration "in coming days" with nationality-based access controls, identity verification for API endpoints, and stricter safety classifiers.

### Key Details
- Mythos 5 (cybersecurity variant) remains restricted to vetted defense orgs under **Project Glasswing**
- Fable 5 restoration will include automatic fallback to Opus 4.8 for sensitive queries in chemistry, biology, cybersecurity
- Anthropic signaled deeper investment in South Korea amid the fallout (Korea JoongAng Daily)

### 📅 Week Ahead Watch
- **Restoration timeline slip?** If Fable 5 isn't restored by end of June, expect significant developer migration to GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.5
- **Congressional hearings** likely on executive branch authority to unilaterally cut off advanced model access
- **Competitor positioning:** OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Meta all watching for market share shifts

## 2. 🏛️ REGULATORY LANDSCAPE

### EU Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA)

**Published:** 3 June as part of EU Tech Sovereignty Package

**Components:**
- **Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA)** — 4-tier cloud sovereignty assurance levels
- **Chips Act 2.0** — expanded semiconductor sovereignty
- **EU Open Source Strategy**
- **Strategic Roadmap for Digitalisation and AI in Energy**

**Impact:** CADA would require EU member states to assess which use cases require specific sovereignty levels that non-EU vendors "would be unable to meet by default" (CCIA Europe critique). This effectively locks US hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) out of the highest assurance tiers.

**Reception:** Mixed. Industry (CCIA Europe) calls it discriminatory. Sovereignty advocates praise it as overdue.

### Norway — Near-Ban on AI in Elementary Schools

**Announced:** 19 June, effective fall 2026
**Scope:** Generative AI tools banned for elementary pupils; restricted use for older children
**Rationale:** PM Støre: AI "could increase the risk that young children miss important steps in their education"
**Broader trend:** First EU/EEA country to implement age-based AI restriction at the institutional level

**📅 Week Ahead:** Other Nordic countries watching Norway closely. Sweden and Finland may follow with similar proposals in Q3 2026.

## 6. 🔗 CROSS-DOMAIN IMPLICATIONS

### Geopolitics ⇄ AI

The Anthropic export control crisis is the defining example of AI as a national security asset. Key takeaways:

1. **Unilateral US export controls on frontier models are now established precedent** — any US-based frontier lab can be cut off from foreign customers by executive action
2. **CADA is a direct response** — EU sovereignty push is accelerating because Europe just watched the US unilaterally shut off advanced models
3. **China's open-weight strategy is vindicated** — GLM-5.2 (MIT License) becomes more attractive as US models become unavailable or unreliable for non-US entities
4. **NVIDIA's Nemotron strategy** — permissive licensing is a hedge against the fragmentation of AI access
