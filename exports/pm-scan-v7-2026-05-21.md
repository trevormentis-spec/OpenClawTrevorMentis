## Hazard Rate Math

Conditional survival between intervals: S(t) = 1 − P(t). Hazard λ over interval Δt:

$$\lambda = -\frac{\ln(S_2/S_1)}{\Delta t}$$

**Today → Jun:** S₁=1.00, S₂=0.895, Δt=10
λ = −ln(0.895)/10 = 0.1109/10 = **1.11%/day**

**Jun → Aug:** S₁=0.895, S₂=0.675, Δt=92
λ = −ln(0.675/0.895)/92 = 0.2817/92 = **0.306%/day**
