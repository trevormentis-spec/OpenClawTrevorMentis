═══ POWER & GRID ═══
PJM continues to be the dominant operational risk for US data centers. The interconnection queue stands at 122 GW — a staggering backlog that has pushed new DC build timelines to 4-6 years. The Northern Virginia cluster (Ashburn, Equinix DC1-DC15) operates at MODERATE risk with a composite score of 56/100, entirely driven by grid constraints. ERCOT (Dallas, Houston) is under high summer load; Equinix DA1 (Infomart) sits at 56/100 grid driver. California ISO (San Jose, LA) faces seasonal flex alerts compounding the risk at SV1/SV5/SV10 and LA1, all 56/100. Energy price spikes are being reported in all three markets during peak hours, shrinking hyperscale margins. The picture is of a US grid that can no longer supply enough firm, dispatchable power to the largest DC concentrations in real time.

═══ NATURAL HAZARD WATCH ═══
Late July means the Atlantic hurricane season is accelerating. The National Hurricane Center is tracking a tropical wave with a 40% chance of development in the next 48 hours. Potential track includes South Florida and the Gulf Coast. Facilities at risk: Equinix MI1 (Miami NOTA), CyrusOne HOU2 (Houston), and the entire Florida colocation corridor. Storm surge and sustained winds could force generator-only operations for 72+ hours — something most Florida facilities are designed for, but fuel logistics remain a wild card. Simultaneously, California wildfire season is active; smoke plumes threaten to choke air intakes at Silicon Valley and LA basin DCs. No mandatory evacuations yet, but San Jose facilities are on alert. Northern Virginia flood risk is baseline for summer; no elevated watch.

═══ MARKET SIGNALS ═══
Transformer lead times just worsened to 18-24 months for >50 MVA units — the single most acute supply-chain bottleneck. Switchgear is at 12-18 months, generators at 8-12 months. The AWS Ohio project alone will require dozens of transformers; at 24-month lead, first power could slip to late 2028 even if permits clear quickly. The takeaway: the US data center market is entering a period of forced geographic diversification — Ohio, Sydney, Frankfurt — while Ashtubula waits for transmission upgrades that may not materialize before 2030.
