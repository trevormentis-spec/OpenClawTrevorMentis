## 4. Confidence Calibration: **7/10**

This is actually the strongest dimension. The analyst is disciplined about capping at "likely" given single-source basis, and explicitly references calibration feedback. That's mature tradecraft.

However:
- The discipline becomes a crutch. Eight of ~15 KJs are "[likely]" — that's not calibration, that's a single-band default. If everything is "likely," the band carries no information.
- **The Senate supplemental KJ ("even chance")** is correctly softer — good.
- **The Hezbollah response KJ ("even chance")** is also appropriately hedged.
- **The MXN underperformance KJ ("even chance")** — this is the right band given the conditional chain (UST move sustains → DXY firms → MXN compresses). Good.
- **Overconfident**: "The IRGC Hormuz exercise will be conducted as a declared drill without a kinetic interdiction" at [likely] — this is closer to *highly likely* historically (IRGC exercises virtually never interdict commercial shipping), but the cap discipline is defensible.

## 5. Prose Quality: **6/10**

Voice is consistent and intelligence-appropriate. "Roderick — overnight..." opener works as a stylistic signature. But:

- **Overlong sentences**: The BLUF opens with a 47-word sentence containing three independent clauses linked by "while" and "—". Break it.
- **Jargon clusters**: "rules-of-engagement ceiling," "deterrence dialogue," "candor-limited finding," "load-bearing caveat" — fine individually, but stacked they become impenetrable.
- **Vague**: "tightens the geopolitical risk premium underneath" — underneath what?
- **Passive constructions**: "is being read as," "will be conducted as," "is being read as signal" — fine in places, but cluster in the BLUF.
- **"Stagflation-lite"**: Cute, but unhelpful. Either it's stagflationary repricing or it isn't.
- **Strong sentences worth preserving**: "A scheduled vote is not a held vote." "Signal direction without signal weight." "The pattern, however, is the analytically useful object." These are the model — direct, declarative, intelligence-appropriate.
