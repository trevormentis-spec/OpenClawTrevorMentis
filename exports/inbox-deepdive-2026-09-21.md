## 3. Pace vs safety — who actually decides
Three separate arguments wearing one coat:
- **Safety:** catastrophic-risk framing (the p(doom) camp; Cato's rebuttal "AI is not about
  to end the world").
- **Control / sovereignty:** who sets the pace. Amodei's call drew Altman and Musk's backing
  and Microsoft's "kill switch" pledge — but Trump publicly mocked it and Beijing called it
  "fearmongering." Taken at face value it is a safety ask; read sceptically (Stratechery) it
  is a lever for political control of AI.
- **Competitive position:** China hands are blunt. Sinocism/Sharp China — "The Limited
  Potential for a Pacing Deal"; Amodei "proposes a misguided framework for forcing Chinese
  compliance"; the CPC's history is about control of technology. The sharpest artefact:
  ChinaTalk ran a viral post by a DeepSeek researcher arguing the end-state is "communism or
  Cyberpunk 2077," and that Anthropic controlling frontier AI "would be no less than letting
  Hitler get the atomic bomb first." In Beijing's framing, pacing = Western incumbency
  protection.
- Also live: US complaints about Chinese "industrial-scale distillation" of US models;
  Anthropic's suggestion that sensitive PRC data reached Claude; a proposed US–China
  "Strategic AI Dialogue" (SAID) as the channel.
- **Bottom line:** the safety argument is real; the *pace* argument is about who holds the
  lever — and both Washington and Beijing read it that way.

## 4. Space Force LEO network — what's actually there
- The flagged item: **SpaceNews, 17 Sep — "Space Force lets SpaceX take the lead in
  designing a new constellation."** Caveat: only the teaser line is in the email; the article
  body has not been retrieved.
- Context that clarifies it: this sits inside SDA (Space Development Agency) work —
  **L3Harris began production on a $955M SDA order**, and the Space Force is making "big
  satellite bets." So "SpaceX leads design of a new constellation" reads as a shift toward
  letting a commercial prime design the architecture, not merely build satellites.
- Around it: **Air Force Secretary Troy Meink revealed the US has on-orbit space-control
  weapons** (first explicit Pentagon statement); **ten US allies agreed to fund a Space Force
  communications satellite**; Texas tapped for a new deep-space radar; **HEO repurposed an
  Earth-observation satellite to approach and inspect a Chinese rocket in orbit**
  (rendezvous-and-proximity operations — a genuine LEO milestone); Europe pushing autonomy
  (von der Leyen on Galileo: capabilities "too important to depend on others"), with
  Project Bromo pitched as a "European champion."
- Bremmer's "Wild West" framing is the right lens: activity far ahead of rules.
