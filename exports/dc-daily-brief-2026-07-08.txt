═══ BLUF ═══
Grid constraints in the US—notably the PJM interconnection backlog (122 GW) and 18–24 month transformer lead times—are the single greatest operational risk to data centers today. Every major Equinix hub in the continental US now sits at a composite risk score of 56/100, driven entirely by power exposure. AWS’s 1200 MW permit filing in Ohio will only deepen the queue. This is not a future concern; it is degrading reliability now.
