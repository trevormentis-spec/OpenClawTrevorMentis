# Hexamine (Hexamethylenetetramine) — Keystone RDX Precursor

**Status:** Supply-constrained — critical leading indicator for RDX production
**Formula:** C6H12N4
**CAS:** 100-97-0

## Role in RDX Production
- Hexamine is the **essential nitrogen backbone** for RDX synthesis via the **Bachmann process** (nitrolysis of hexamine with nitric acid and ammonium nitrate)
- Without hexamine, RDX cannot be manufactured through the standard industrial process
- **~2.5-3 kg hexamine required per kg of RDX** (varies by process efficiency)

## Global Supply

### US Market
- **Only domestic producer:** Bakelite LLC (Atlanta, Georgia) — sole US supplier of granular hexamine
- Major import sources (pre-2022): Russia, China, Germany, India, Saudi Arabia
- **Russian supply collapse:** Russia redirected hexamine from export to military RDX production in 2022-2023 — major supply shock

### Bakelite Anti-Dumping Case
- **2024-10-03:** Bakelite filed AD/CVD petition against China, Germany, India, Saudi Arabia
- **2025-09-08:** USITC preliminary affirmative: "US industry threatened with material injury" — imports from China found sold at LTFV and subsidized
- **Expected outcome:** Final affirmative determination by mid-2026 → tariffs imposed on Chinese hexamine
- **Paradox:** Tariffs protect domestic producer but raise costs for RDX manufacturing — unless Bakelite directly supplies Holston

### Global Hexamine Supply Chain
- **Russia:** Metafrax (Perm Krai) — major hexamine producer; struck Feb 2026
- **China:** World's largest hexamine producer; dominant in global trade
- **Germany:** Chemical sector (BASF, others) — European supply hub
- **India:** Significant producer — Solar Industries uses captive consumption
- **Saudi Arabia:** Petrochemical-based hexamine
