# Solar Industries India Limited (SIIL)

**Status:** Active — India's leading defense explosives manufacturer, expanding rapidly
**Region:** Asia-Pacific
**Location:** Nagpur, Maharashtra, India (HQ); Chakdoh & multiple plants
**Ticker:** BSE:532725 / NSE:SOLARINDS

## Operator
- Solar Industries India Ltd.
- Subsidiaries in Australia, Indonesia, South Africa

## Products (Defense)
- **RDX and RDX compositions** (military-grade)
- **HMX and HMX compositions**
- **TNT**
- Composite propellants (Pinaka, Akash, Brahmos, PSOMXL, Skyroot)
- Warhead filling, pyros ignitors
- Industrial explosives (slurry, emulsion, cast booster)

## Capacity
- Chakdoh plant: established 1995, produces multiple explosive types
- India's sole private-sector RDX/HMX producer
- Expanding defense production capacity through government's Atmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliance) program
- Exports to 25+ countries

## Key Developments
- **2015:** Commenced HMX supply
- **2016:** Set up warhead filling, propellant for Pinaka Mk-II
- **2016:** Manufacturing facility in South Africa
- **2020s:** Major expansion of defense explosives capacity driven by Indian military modernization

## Strategic Importance
- India's primary private-sector producer of military-grade RDX and HMX
- Indian military is one of world's largest ammunition consumers
- Self-sufficiency push reduces dependency on Chinese and Russian precursors
- Key supplier for Indian artillery and rocket programs (Pinaka, Akash)
- Growing export ambition in the Southeast Asian defense market
