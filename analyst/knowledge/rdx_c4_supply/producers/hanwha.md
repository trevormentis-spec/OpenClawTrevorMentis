# Hanwha Corporation (Hanwha Group)

**Status:** Active — Major South Korean defense/explosives conglomerate
**Region:** Asia-Pacific
**Location:** Seoul, South Korea (HQ); multiple industrial sites
**Ticker:** KRX:000880

## Operator
- Hanwha Corporation / Hanwha Group
- Founded 1952 as **Korea Explosives Co.** — origins in explosives manufacturing

## Products (Defense/Energetics)
- RDX and RDX compositions
- HMX and HMX compositions
- TNT
- Propellants (solid rocket, artillery)
- Industrial explosives
- Defense systems (artillery, munitions)

## Capacity
- South Korea's #1 commercial explosives provider
- Multiple production lines across Korean industrial complexes
- Active export market penetration (cost advantages + government financing)
- Expanding production capacity for NATO-standard 155mm shells

## Strategic Importance
- Hanwha is aggressively expanding artillery ammunition production — building new 155mm production lines for export
- Hanwha Aerospace signed major NATO ammunition supply deals
- Asian challenger using cost advantages to penetrate export markets previously dominated by Western incumbents
- Strong government backing for defense export expansion
