# Rheinmetall Várpalota (Rheinmetall Hungary Munitions Zrt.)

**Status:** Commissioning — New ammunition production facility
**Region:** Europe
**Location:** Várpalota, Veszprém County, Hungary
**Coordinates:** 47.20°N, 18.14°E (approx)

## Operator
- Rheinmetall AG (joint venture with Hungarian state-owned N7 Holding)
- Ticker: XTRA:RHN

## Products
- 155mm artillery shells (PzH 2000 / NATO standard)
- 120mm tank ammunition (Leopard 2)
- Medium-caliber ammunition
- Explosives/munition filling

## Capacity
- Site: ~150 hectares
- Production line 1 (medium-caliber): operational in early 2026
- Production line 2 (155mm): scheduled for early 2026
- Multiple expansion phases planned (part of ongoing capacity growth)

## Key Events
- **Construction:** Built under Hungary's Zrinyi 2026 military modernization program
- **2024-01:** Expansion announced to include 155mm shells
- **2025-11:** Rheinmetall holding website describes 150-hectare production center
- **2026-01:** Production slated to begin early 2026
- **2026 ongoing:** Capacity expansion continues; development and acceptance testing

## Strategic Importance
- Part of Rheinmetall's massive European ammunition capacity expansion (new facilities in Germany, Romania, Lithuania, Hungary)
- Direct supply to Hungarian Armed Forces under Zrinyi 2026
- Rheinmetall positioning: largest European ammunition producer
- Hungary geographically central for NATO logistics
- **Not an RDX producer per se** — consumes RDX/TNT for munition filling
