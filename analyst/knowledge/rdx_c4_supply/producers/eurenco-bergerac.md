# Eurenco Bergerac

**Status:** Active — European RDX and propellant anchor
**Region:** Europe
**Location:** Bergerac, Dordogne, France
**Coordinates:** 44.85°N, 0.49°E (approx)

## Operator
- Eurenco (European leader in energetic materials; state-backed French company)

## Products
- RDX
- Nitrocellulose (NC) — for propellant charges
- Large-caliber artillery propellants (155mm)
- Single-base and double-base propellants

## Capacity
- Nitrocellulose: production restarted after accident (see below)
- New large-caliber propellant line: construction completed, operational Jan 2025
- Sorgues site (also Eurenco): can coatings, fuses

## Key Events
- **2022-08-03:** NC production shut down after accident damaged installation
- **2024-04-11:** France commits €500M ($540M) to restart artillery propellant production; resume explosive powder production
- **2024:** NC production restarted after safety refurbishment
- **2025-01:** New gunpowder production line started operations for artillery projectiles
- **2025:** Relocation of large-caliber propellant production to Bergerac (from Sweden)

## Strategic Importance
- **European RDX anchor** — one of few European RDX producers
- Critical to EU rearmament push (2M shells/year target by end-2025)
- NC restart essential for maintaining European artillery propellant supply
- 70%+ of European NC cotton linters sourced from China — strategic vulnerability
