# Holston Army Ammunition Plant (HSAAP)

**Status:** Active — US sole RDX/HMX producer
**Region:** North America
**Location:** Kingsport, Tennessee, USA
**Coordinates:** 36.53°N, 82.55°W (approx)
**Opened:** 1943 (original), continuously modernized

## Operator
- BAE Systems Ordnance Systems Inc. (GOCO contract through 2033)
- Owned by US Army Joint Munitions Command

## Products
- RDX (Research Department Explosive / Cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine)
- HMX (High Melting Explosive / Cyclotetramethylenetetranitramine)
- Composition B (RDX/TNT/wax blend)
- Fluids Energy Mill (FEM) RDX

## Capacity
- Current RDX: ~8M lbs/year (~3,600 tonnes/year)
- Planned expansion: ~15M lbs/year (~6,800 tonnes/year)
- FEM RDX: Two new production trains being built; ~4M lbs material at any time
- Weak Acetic Acid Recovery Plant: 1943-built, 10,769 sq ft — being modernized

## Key Events
- **2024-05-01:** Army article on modernization efforts; FEM trains under construction
- **2025-01-16:** Army ammunition plant modernization plan announced — RDX/HMX upgrades prioritized
- **2026-03-19:** "Plant X" expansion concept documented in NTRL report — new standalone RDX/HMX facility
- **Ongoing:** FEMA capacity expansion, FEM mill upgrades

## Strategic Importance
- **Single point of failure for US RDX supply** — no other domestic RDX producer
- Bottleneck for 155mm artillery shell production (100K/month target)
- Plant X concept would create second US RDX line, but years away from operational
