# Sverdlov Plant (Ya.M. Sverdlov Plant)

**Status:** Severely damaged — struck by Ukrainian drone April 30, 2026
**Region:** Russia
**Location:** Dzerzhinsk, Nizhny Novgorod Oblast, Russia
**Coordinates:** 56.24°N, 43.47°E (approx)
**Operator:** Russian state-owned defense industry

## Products
- RDX (Russia's **sole** domestic producer)
- HMX (Octogen — Russia's sole producer per Ukrainian intelligence)
- TNT
- Other military explosives
- Industrial explosives

## Capacity
- Total annual production (incl. branches): ~8,000 tonnes explosives
- Russia's largest explosives manufacturer

## Key Events
- **2026-04-30:** Ukrainian drone strike hits production buildings — **large fire confirmed**
- **2026-05 (early):** Satellite imagery confirms damage to production buildings
- **2026-05-08:** Ukrainian General Staff confirms strike on Sverdlov Plant
- **Confirmation sources:** Satellite imagery (CyberBoroshno, Dnipro OSINT), Ukrainian General Staff, UNITED24 Media

## Strategic Importance
- **Single source of RDX and HMX for entire Russian military** — no backup facility
- April 2026 strike likely destroyed/disabled critical production lines
- Repairs constrained by Western sanctions on equipment, components, and technical expertise
- Russia had been converting hexamine capacity to military RDX since 2022 — any RDX production loss is effectively irreplaceable
- Russian forces already expending artillery at high rates in Ukraine — supply gap has immediate battlefield impact
- Supporting facilities (Promsintez, Bryansk, Dorogobuzh, Metafrax) also under systematic Ukrainian attack
