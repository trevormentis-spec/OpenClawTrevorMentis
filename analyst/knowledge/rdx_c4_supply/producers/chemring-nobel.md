# Chemring Nobel

**Status:** Active — One of only two European advanced military explosives manufacturers
**Region:** Europe
**Location:** Saetre, Norway (near Oslo)
**Coordinates:** 59.68°N, 10.52°E (approx)

## Operator
- Chemring Group PLC (UK-listed defence contractor)
- Ticker: LON:CHG

## Products
- RDX (Cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine)
- HMX (High Melting Explosive)
- NTO (Nitrotriazolone)
- Insensitive munition formulations

## Capacity
- Current: existing production capacity
- **Expansion underway:** £32M co-funded by Norwegian government + EU → more than double existing capacity
- **Feasibility study (concluded late 2024):** New production facility at different location under consideration

## Key Events
- **2024-10-10:** Norwegian Government announces measures to strengthen defence industry; co-funds Chemring Nobel £32M
- **2024-10-13:** Feasibility study launched for new production facility (concluded by end 2024)
- **2025-09-19:** Feasibility study results — evaluation of location, infrastructure, environmental factors

## Strategic Importance
- **One of only two European manufacturers of advanced military explosives** (alongside Eurenco)
- Expanding capacity critical for NATO artillery ammunition surge
- RDX, HMX, NTO used in insensitive munitions — growing mandate in NATO standards
- Norway shares border with Russia in Arctic — strategic supply assurance
