# Nitro-Chem (Grupa Azoty Nitro-Chem)

**Status:** Active — Sole NATO TNT producer, rapidly expanding
**Region:** Europe
**Location:** Bydgoszcz, Poland
**Coordinates:** 53.12°N, 18.01°E (approx)

## Operator
- Grupa Azoty (state-controlled Polish chemical group)
- Owned via Grupa Azoty Zakłady Chemiczne "Nitro-Chem" S.A.

## Products
- **TNT** (Trinitrotoluene) — primary product
- Other military explosives
- Nitric acid, sulphuric acid
- Industrial chemicals

## Capacity
- Europe's largest TNT plant; **sole NATO TNT production facility in Europe**
- Current: existing single production line
- Expansion: **second production unit** announced March 2026 at Bydgoszcz site
- No capacity expansion needed for recent US contract (existing infrastructure adequate per management)

## Key Events
- **2020s:** Sustained max-capacity operations due to Ukraine war demand
- **2025-04:** Signed major TNT delivery deal for US artillery ammunition production (existing capacity)
- **2026-03-14:** Announced plans to build new second production line
- **2026-03-18:** Expansion formally confirmed — unprecedented peacetime TNT capacity build
- **2026-04-14:** Defense News covers Swedish-Polish TNT investment surge

## Strategic Importance
- **Single point of failure for NATO TNT** — only plant in alliance producing TNT (Swebal AB in Sweden building new plant targeting 2028)
- Direct supplier to US 155mm production line
- TNT used in both RDX-based fills (Composition B = 60% RDX + 40% TNT) and standalone munitions
- Poland rearming rapidly (4% GDP defense spending)
