# SSC Space (Swedish Space Corporation)

## Overview
- **Type:** Tier-1 Heritage GSaaS (State-owned)
- **HQ:** Solna, Sweden
- **Ownership:** 100% Swedish state via Ministry of Education
- **Founded:** 1972
- **Employees:** 713

## Network
- **Sites:** 21 (10 owned + 11 partner)
- **Antennas:** 100+
- **Key Sites:** Esrange Space Center (Kiruna), Inuvik, Singapore, Punta Arenas, Santiago, TrollSat, Clemsford

## Financials
- **Revenue:** SEK 1.7B (~$160M)
- **Funding:** State-funded

## Key Differentiator
Only GSaaS operator with launch capability (Esrange Space Center). Deep space tracking capability. Swedish sovereign space infrastructure.

## Strategic Position
Sweden's national space infrastructure provider. Esrange provides both launch and ground segment services. European strategic autonomy asset. Growing commercial GSaaS business alongside sovereign mission support.
