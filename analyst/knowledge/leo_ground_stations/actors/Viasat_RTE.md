# Viasat Real-Time Earth (RTE)

## Overview
- **Type:** Tier-4 Hybrid / Integrated Operator
- **HQ:** Carlsbad, California, USA
- **Ownership:** Viasat Inc (NASDAQ: VSAT)
- **Founded:** 2019

## Network
- **Sites:** 10
- **Antennas:** 10

## Financials
- **Revenue:** Part of Viasat ($2.3B total revenue)
- **Ownership:** Public company

## Business Model
High-throughput owned infrastructure + satellite relay. Real-Time Earth service for low-latency data delivery.

## Key Differentiator
Public company GSaaS offering. Infostellar StellarStation co-location partnership. Space42/Equatys JV for Middle East coverage.

## Strategic Position
Part of larger Viasat satellite communications group. Not pure-play GSaaS — benefits from Viasat's established satellite operator relationships and balance sheet.
