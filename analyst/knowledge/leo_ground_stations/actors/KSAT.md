# KSAT (Kongsberg Satellite Services)

## Overview
- **Type:** Tier-1 Heritage GSaaS (State-controlled)
- **HQ:** Tromsø, Norway
- **Ownership:** Space Norway AS (50%) + Kongsberg Defence (50%) — both Norwegian state-controlled
- **Incorporated:** Norway
- **Founded:** 2002
- **Employees:** ~270

## Network
- **Sites:** 26+
- **Antennas:** 280+
- **Satellites Supported:** 150
- **Key Sites:** Svalbard (78°N — polar monopoly), Troll Station Antarctica (72°S), Singapore, Alaska, Fairbanks, Inuvik, Athens, Dubai
- **Traffic Tier (Concentric):** Peak (5) — Svalbard/Tromsø sites

## Financials
- **Revenue:** ~$200M (estimated)
- **Implied Revenue per Antenna:** ~$714K
- **Funding:** Norwegian state-backed

## Key Differentiator
Polar monopoly — only operator with permanent ground stations at both poles (Svalbard 78°N, Troll 72°S). 50+ year heritage in polar satellite operations. Serves NATO governments and US defence agencies.

## Concentric Assessment
> "KSAT, the world's largest GSaaS provider, is 100% Norwegian state-controlled through Space Norway and Kongsberg. Norway's unique geographic position (Svalbard at 78°N, Troll Antarctica at 72°S) gives it an irreplaceable polar coverage monopoly."

**Security implications:** The state ownership structures of KSAT (and SSC) mean their government/defence customers already operate within a trusted national security framework. A partnership with KSAT would position within a Five Eyes / NATO-aligned ecosystem.

**Concentric entry point:** Physical Security to GSaaS Providers — KSAT (26 sites, NATO/defence clients, Norwegian state-backed) is a priority target for per-site security assessment, ongoing monitoring, guard force and hardening services.

## Recent Developments
- **Feb 2026:** Announced KSAT Hyper — space-based relay (orbiting ground stations) extending polar monopoly into orbit. Concentric assessment: "May partially reduce polar ground dependency but will not eliminate it."
- **Jan 2026:** Joined AWS GSaaS Partner Programme
- **2024:** Expanding with new sites in Southeast Asia and Africa

## Strategic Position
Critical national infrastructure for Norway. De facto monopoly for high-latitude polar passes. All LEO constellations with polar orbits depend on KSAT for pole-to-pole coverage. KSAT Hyper represents a paradigm shift — extending ground capacity into orbit.
