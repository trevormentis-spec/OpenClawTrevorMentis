# Lloyd's LEO Ground Station Insurance Market — May 2026
**Source:** Concentric Intelligence Memorandum (2026-05-18)
**Added:** 2026-05-21

## Market Structure
No single Lloyd's syndicate writes stand-alone "LEO ground-station" policies.
Ground stations are insured through a stack of four parallel risk classes:
1. **Space TPL / space-segment** — third-party liability for RF interference, spectrum bleed
2. **Terrestrial property / all-risks** — physical damage to dishes, radomes, electronics, BI from site loss
3. **Cyber & space-segment cyber** — jamming, spoofing, command override, modem-wiper events
4. **Political violence / terrorism** — sabotage, terrorism, SRCC, war (where bought back)

## Market Dynamics
- **Capacity is tight and shrinking** — hasn't returned to 2018 levels; 2023 worst space-line result in 20+ years
- **~99% of LEO satellites uninsured** — operators rely on in-orbit redundancy
- **Ground stations are the actual buy** — single gateway loss is not redundant
- **KA-SAT attack (24 Feb 2022)** — canonical ground-segment cyber loss scenario; modem-wiper via misconfigured VPN
- **LMA5567A/B state-backed cyber war exclusions** (March 2023) — on every cyber slip touching satellite ground
- **Space-segment cyber** — manuscript product bridging space + cyber boxes

## Implications for Concentric / Kessler Sentinel
1. **Buyer is the broker, not the syndicate** — Marsh, Aon ISB, Gallagher, WTW
2. **Named underwriter map is small** — ~12 people; can cover in one London week
3. **Cyber + PV/terror are the higher-conviction wedges** — not pure space-class
4. **Addressable underwriter list extends beyond space-only bench** — includes property, cyber, PV syndicates
